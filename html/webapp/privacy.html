Epic Ball Adventure Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 21, 2026

Epic Ball Adventure does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data.

The game stores level progress locally on the player's device only. It does not use accounts, ads, analytics, location, camera, microphone, contacts, or other sensitive permissions.

Local progress can be removed by clearing the app data or uninstalling the app.
