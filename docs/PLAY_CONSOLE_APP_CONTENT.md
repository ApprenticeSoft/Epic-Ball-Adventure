# Google Play App Content Draft

Use this as the working source for Play Console > Policy > App content. The final Play Console forms still need to be completed in the account UI.

## Privacy Policy

- URL: https://ball.marcvidal.ca/privacy.html
- Status gate: `npm run verify:play-store-live` must pass before submission.
- In-app access: Main menu Privacy overlay.

## App Access

- Restricted access: No.
- Login required: No.
- Account creation: No.
- Purchases required: No.
- Instructions for review: No credentials or special setup are required. Open the app, tap or press the one-button control to start, and play the bundled levels.

## Ads

- Contains ads: No.
- Ad SDKs: None.

## Data Safety

- Data collected: No user data collected.
- Data shared: No user data shared.
- Data processed ephemerally: No.
- Data encrypted in transit: Not applicable; the app does not transmit user data.
- Data deletion request mechanism: Not applicable because the app has no accounts and collects no user data. Local progress can be removed by clearing app data or uninstalling the app.
- Data types: None.
- Purposes: None.

## Target Audience And Content

- Target age groups: 13-15, 16-17, 18+.
- Designed primarily for children under 13: No.
- App access restrictions: None.
- Personal information collection from children: None.
- Store presence: Not primarily child-directed. The listing positions the game as a compact physics platformer for short, focused play sessions.
- Neutral age screen: Not applicable because the app does not target children under 13 and has no ads, account features, purchases, or user interaction.

## Content Rating Draft

- Category: Game.

- Sexual content or nudity: None.

## Notes

- Google Play assigns the final regional content ratings through the IARC questionnaire.
- If gameplay, monetization, ads, account features, analytics, or online features are added later, update this draft, the privacy policy, Data safety answers, and the content rating questionnaire before shipping.
